Real market. Real yield.

Ethics Launch pairs new tokens with real quote assets instead of a synthetic curve. $ETHICSTR takes $ETHICS as its quote, with liquidity locked at launch, so the fee stream is denominated in the token you actually want to accumulate.

i

Trade happens

Someone buys or sells $ETHICSTR in the $ETHICSTR / $ETHICS pool on Ethics Launch. The pool runs in Rewards mode with a 2% fee.

ii

Fee is collected in $ETHICS

Instant DAMMv2 fees are quote-only. Because $ETHICS is the quote asset, the whole 2% accrues in $ETHICS and routes to the Rewards vault.

iii

Vault pays holders

Every 2 hours (UTC) the vault claims the accumulated fees and airdrops 90% to $ETHICSTR holders as $ETHICS. Ethics keeps 10%. Nothing to claim.

Questions

Do I have to stake or claim anything?

No. The Ethics Rewards vault airdrops $ETHICS straight to the wallets holding $ETHICSTR at each epoch. Keep the tokens in a wallet you control so the snapshot can see them.

Why is the payout in $ETHICS and not $ETHICSTR?

The pool's fees are quote-only, and $ETHICS is the quote asset. That means holders accumulate the platform token rather than more of the token they already hold — the "strategy" is a position in $ETHICS that funds itself through trading volume.

How often do airdrops happen?

Every two hours on the UTC clock. The counter at the top of this page shows the time to the next one. A quiet two-hour window with no trades simply pays out nothing that cycle.

What is the "strategy" in strategy token?

A strategy token is a token whose purpose is to accumulate a specific asset for its holders. $ETHICSTR is built to accumulate $ETHICS. Volume in, $ETHICS out.

Is liquidity locked?

Yes — permanently. The Instant DAMMv2 pool is one-sided and locked; liquidity cannot be withdrawn, and mint and freeze authority are disabled. Verify it on the token page at ethics.ltd.
